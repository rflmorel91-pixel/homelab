## Prospecting Rules

- Start with people/businesses already known or reachable through personal networks.
- Focus on businesses with obvious infrastructure needs.
- Do not promise work before completing an assessment.
- Do not provide unrestricted technical access before an engagement is approved.
- Keep the first project small and clearly scoped.
- Document every interaction and next action.
