# SaaS Customer Discovery

## Product Hypothesis

JobFlow is a proposed SaaS platform for independent home-service professionals.

The initial hypothesis is that small handyman businesses lose time managing:

- Customer requests
- Photos and job information
- Estimates
- Scheduling
- Customer follow-ups
- Invoices
- Payments

The product has not been validated yet.

Customer interviews will determine whether this problem is significant enough to build a SaaS product around.

---

## Ideal Customer Profile

Initial target:

- Independent handyman businesses
- 1–5 employees
- Residential customers
- Approximately 10–50 jobs per month
- Heavy use of phone/text communication
- Manual or disconnected administrative workflows

---

## Customer Discovery Rules

1. Do not pitch the product during discovery interviews.
2. Ask customers to describe their existing workflow.
3. Record problems in the customer's own words.
4. Determine how frequently each problem occurs.
5. Determine how much time the problem consumes.
6. Identify existing workarounds.
7. Identify existing software and its cost.
8. Look for evidence of willingness to pay.
9. Do not build a feature solely because a customer says it sounds useful.
10. Build only after a repeated painful problem has been validated.

---

## Core Interview Questions

1. How do new customers normally contact you?
2. When someone sends you a job request, what information do you collect?
3. Do customers send you pictures or videos? How do you handle those?
4. How do you create and send estimates?
5. How do you keep track of estimates that have not been approved?
6. How do you schedule jobs?
7. What happens when a customer needs to reschedule?
8. How do you create and track invoices?
9. What part of managing jobs takes the most time or causes the most headaches?
10. If you could eliminate one administrative task from your business, what would it be?

---

## Follow-up Questions

When a customer identifies a problem:

- How often does that happen?
- How much time does that take?
- How do you handle it today?
- Have you tried software to solve it?
- What does the current solution cost?
- What do you dislike about the current solution?
- What happens when the problem is not handled correctly?

# Validation Decision

The SaaS will move forward only when customer interviews demonstrate a repeated and meaningful problem.

Target validation:

- At least 5 customer interviews
- At least 3 customers reporting the same core problem
- Clear evidence that the problem consumes time or money
- Existing workaround identified
- Evidence that customers would consider paying for a better solution

Until these conditions are met, major application development should remain paused.

---

# Product Development Principle

Build → Measure → Learn.

Customer evidence determines the product.

Infrastructure and software development should support validated customer demand rather than replace customer discovery.
