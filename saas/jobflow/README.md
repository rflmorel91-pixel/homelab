# JobFlow

JobFlow is a SaaS product experiment for independent home-service businesses.

## Current Phase

Customer discovery and prototype validation.

## Problem Hypothesis

Small service businesses may manage customers, jobs, estimates, scheduling, and invoices across disconnected tools.

## Prototype Workflow

Customer → Job → Estimate → Approval → Schedule → Complete → Invoice → Paid

## Initial Goal

Build a small disposable prototype that can be demonstrated to prospective customers.

The prototype is not production software.

## Validation Rule

Do not expand the product until customer interviews demonstrate a recurring problem worth solving.

## Planned MVP Areas

- Customer management
- Job management
- Estimates
- Scheduling
- Invoicing

## Explicitly Deferred

- Payments
- Accounting integrations
- AI
- Mobile application
- Advanced reporting
- Complex permissions
- Enterprise features
