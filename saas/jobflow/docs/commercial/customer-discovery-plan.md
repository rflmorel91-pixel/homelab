# JobFlow Customer Discovery Plan

Version: 1.0

---

# Block 1 — Customer Discovery Status

## Current Validation Status

JobFlow is currently in the customer discovery phase.

No completed customer discovery interviews have been conducted at this time.

## Product Validation Position

Current status:

- Product concept defined
- Technical MVP developed
- Backend SaaS architecture implemented
- Customer validation pending

## Purpose

The purpose of customer discovery is to validate:

- Whether the identified problem exists
- Whether target customers experience sufficient pain
- Whether the proposed workflow provides value
- Whether customers would consider using or paying for JobFlow

---

# Block 2 — Current Product Hypothesis

## Target Customer Hypothesis

JobFlow is initially designed for:

- Handyman businesses
- Cleaning companies
- Landscaping companies
- Independent contractors
- Small home-service providers

## Problem Hypothesis

Small service businesses may struggle with:

- Managing customer information
- Tracking job progress
- Creating estimates
- Scheduling work
- Managing invoices and payments

## Solution Hypothesis

JobFlow proposes a unified workflow platform connecting:

Customer → Job → Estimate → Approval → Schedule → Completion → Invoice → Payment

## Validation Requirement

These assumptions must be validated through direct customer conversations before making commercial decisions.

---

# Block 3 — Customer Discovery Interview Framework

## Interview Goal

The purpose of customer interviews is to understand real operational problems experienced by small home-service businesses.

Interviews should focus on learning rather than selling.

## Discovery Questions

### Current Workflow

Questions:

- How do you currently manage customer requests?
- How do you track jobs from request to completion?
- How do you create and send estimates?
- How do you schedule work?
- How do you track invoices and payments?

### Current Challenges

Questions:

- What parts of your workflow take the most time?
- What information is difficult to keep organized?
- What tools do you currently use?
- What problems occur when managing multiple jobs?

### Software Usage

Questions:

- Do you currently use any business management software?
- What do you like or dislike about your current tools?
- What would make a new tool valuable?

### Purchase Validation

Questions:

- How would solving this problem affect your business?
- Would you pay for a tool that improves this workflow?
- What features would be most important?

# Block 4 — Evidence Tracking

Customer discovery evidence should record:

- Date of conversation
- Customer type
- Business size
- Current workflow
- Problems identified
- Requested features
- Willingness to evaluate JobFlow

## Validation Rule

Customer feedback should be documented separately from product assumptions.

Confirmed customer needs should be based on direct conversations or measurable evidence.

---

# Block 5 — Customer Validation Decision Criteria

## Purpose

Customer discovery results should guide product decisions before commercial launch.

## Validation Signals

Positive validation signals may include:

- Customers describing the problem without prompting
- Existing workflow causing measurable frustration
- Interest in testing JobFlow
- Requests for specific workflow improvements
- Willingness to discuss pricing

## Negative Validation Signals

Possible negative signals include:

- Problem is not significant
- Existing tools already solve the problem
- Customers are unwilling to change workflows
- Customers do not see enough value

## Decision Process

Customer feedback should influence:

- Feature priorities
- Product positioning
- Pricing strategy
- Target customer selection
- Go-to-market approach

## Commercial Principle

JobFlow should be developed based on validated customer needs rather than assumptions alone.
