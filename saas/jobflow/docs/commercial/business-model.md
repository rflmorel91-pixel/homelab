# Block 1 — Business Model Status and Revenue Hypothesis

## Current Business Model Status

JobFlow is currently in the product development and customer discovery phase.

No commercial pricing model has been validated with customers.

## Business Model Purpose

This document records business model assumptions that require validation before commercial launch.

## Target Customer

Initial target customers:

- Handyman businesses
- Cleaning companies
- Landscaping companies
- Independent contractors
- Small home-service providers

## Revenue Hypothesis

JobFlow may operate as a subscription-based SaaS product.

Potential subscription model:

- Monthly recurring subscription
- Tiered feature plans
- Optional premium capabilities

## Value Hypothesis

Customers may pay for JobFlow if it provides measurable value through:

- Reduced administrative work
- Better job organization
- Improved customer tracking
- More reliable estimate and payment workflows

## Validation Requirement

Pricing and willingness-to-pay assumptions must be validated through:

- Customer interviews
- Product demonstrations
- Trial usage
- Market feedback

Current status:

Business model hypothesis only.
Not commercially validated.

# Block 2 — Pricing Hypothesis and Packaging Strategy

## Pricing Status

No final pricing has been established.

Pricing decisions should be based on:

- Customer value
- Customer willingness to pay
- Competitive alternatives
- Operating costs
- Product maturity

## Initial Pricing Hypothesis

JobFlow may use a subscription pricing model.

Possible structure:

### Starter Plan

Designed for individual service providers.

Possible features:

- Customer management
- Job tracking
- Estimate workflow
- Basic scheduling

### Business Plan

Designed for growing service businesses.

Possible features:

- Multiple users
- Advanced workflow features
- Reporting capabilities
- Additional automation

### Premium Plan

Designed for businesses requiring advanced capabilities.

Possible features:

- Enhanced reporting
- Integrations
- Priority support
- Additional business tools

## Pricing Validation Requirements

Before commercial launch:

- Discuss pricing with potential customers
- Test willingness to pay
- Compare alternatives
- Adjust packaging based on evidence

## Current Status

Pricing structure is a business hypothesis.

No pricing plan is considered final until validated.

# Block 3 — Revenue Model Risks and Validation Metrics

## Business Model Risks

Potential risks requiring validation:

- Customers may not consider the problem urgent enough
- Existing tools may already satisfy customer needs
- Customers may not accept subscription pricing
- Customer acquisition costs may exceed expected value
- Required support effort may affect profitability

## Validation Metrics

Future validation should track:

### Customer Interest

- Number of discovery conversations
- Customer requests for evaluation
- Demo interest

### Product Value

- Features customers identify as important
- Workflow problems reported
- Time savings identified

### Commercial Interest

- Pricing feedback
- Willingness-to-pay signals
- Trial participation
- Conversion interest

## Decision Principle

Business model decisions should be based on customer evidence and measurable results.

## Current Status

JobFlow business model remains a hypothesis under validation.
