# RenewalDesk 30-Day Pilot Offer

Version: 1.0
Status: Ready for qualified prospects
Last updated: 2026-08-21

---

## 1. Offer Summary

RenewalDesk helps small organizations track licenses, insurance
policies, contracts, subscriptions, certifications, and other renewal
deadlines in one workspace.

Initial commercial offer:

- Price: `$99 USD`
- Billing: One-time
- Duration: 30 calendar days
- Product: RenewalDesk
- Workspace: One organization tenant
- Initial owner: One
- Standard onboarding: Included
- Pilot review: Required
- Automatic renewal: Disabled
- Subscription commitment: None
- Custom development: Excluded

The price is a commercial hypothesis, not validated permanent pricing.

---

## 2. Demand Assumption

The operator has chosen to proceed under the assumption that a market
need exists for RenewalDesk.

This assumption allows the project to enter packaging and sales
experimentation without completing the original customer-interview
threshold.

It does not constitute evidence of:

- Product-market fit
- Willingness to pay
- Repeatable customer acquisition
- Customer retention
- Validated pricing
- A validated target segment

Actual purchase, use, retention, and customer outcomes must be recorded
separately from this assumption.

---

## 3. Intended Customer

The pilot is intended for a small organization where one person manages
multiple recurring obligations without dedicated procurement or
compliance software.

Examples include:

- Small businesses
- Property managers
- Independent professional offices
- Nonprofit organizations
- Small IT service providers
- Consultants managing client renewals

The strongest initial fit is an organization currently relying on
spreadsheets, calendars, email, notes, vendor portals, or staff memory.

---

## 4. Problem Addressed

RenewalDesk is intended to reduce the risk and administrative effort
associated with:

- Missed renewal deadlines
- Last-minute renewal work
- Late fees
- Service interruptions
- Expired licenses or certifications
- Unclear ownership
- Fragmented renewal information
- Reliance on one employee's memory

These outcomes remain product hypotheses until observed in customer
use.

---

## 5. Included Capabilities

The pilot includes:

- A private organization workspace
- Renewal record creation and editing
- Renewal categories and vendors
- Renewal dates and status
- Cost tracking
- Renewal ownership
- Owner email addresses
- Configurable reminder lead times
- Dashboard summaries
- Urgency indicators
- Search and filtering
- Persistent reminder processing
- Email reminder delivery
- Reminder delivery history
- Standard assisted onboarding
- Reasonable pilot support
- End-of-pilot review

---

## 6. Excluded Capabilities

The pilot does not include:

- Custom feature development
- Custom integrations
- SMS or mobile notifications
- Native mobile applications
- Enterprise procurement workflows
- Advanced role customization
- Guaranteed data imports
- Guaranteed continuous availability
- A service-level agreement
- Automatic renewal
- Recurring subscription billing
- Production deployment guarantees

Requested changes are recorded as evidence and are not commitments.

---

## 7. Customer Responsibilities

The participant agrees to:

- Identify one pilot owner
- Provide accurate pilot information
- Protect login credentials
- Enter at least five realistic or real renewal records
- Use the workspace during the pilot
- Report material problems
- Participate in the final pilot review
- Provide honest positive and negative feedback
- Make an explicit continuation decision

The participant must not enter payment credentials, highly sensitive
personal information, secrets, or unrelated confidential data into
free-text fields.

---

## 8. Standard Onboarding

Standard onboarding includes:

1. Confirm the organization and pilot owner.
2. Confirm the pilot use case.
3. Agree on start and end dates.
4. Record measurable success criteria.
5. Confirm payment when applicable.
6. Provision one RenewalDesk tenant.
7. Confirm owner access.
8. Enter the first renewal record.
9. Configure one reminder.
10. Schedule the pilot review.

Custom implementation work is not included.

---

## 9. Pilot Success Criteria

The standard pilot targets the following outcomes:

- At least five renewal records are entered
- At least three renewal categories are represented
- At least one owner email is configured
- At least one reminder is successfully delivered
- The participant uses RenewalDesk during at least two weeks
- The participant can find upcoming and overdue renewals
- The participant identifies a specific reduction in effort or risk
- The participant completes the final review
- The participant makes a continuation decision

Success is not established merely because the software remains online
or the participant likes the interface.

---

## 10. Qualification

The offer may be presented publicly, but provisioning still requires:

- A real prospect
- A relevant renewal-management use case
- An identified pilot owner
- Agreed success criteria
- Agreed pilot dates
- Understood support boundaries
- An active platform user
- A qualified platform lead
- Satisfaction of the applicable billing gate

The operator's demand assumption does not permit unsafe or anonymous
tenant provisioning.

---

## 11. Billing

The `$99` pilot is invoiced manually through the platform's approved
Stripe procedure.

Requirements:

- Payment is due before paid-pilot provisioning
- The invoice identifies RenewalDesk
- Automatic recurring billing is disabled
- Payment status is verified directly in Stripe
- Payment credentials are never stored in the repository
- Customer billing details remain private

No product-specific billing integration will be created.

---

## 12. Pilot Completion

Before the end date:

1. Review usage against the success criteria.
2. Record successful and unsuccessful outcomes.
3. Record onboarding and support effort.
4. Ask whether the customer wants to continue.
5. Discuss an acceptable future price and billing period.
6. Decide whether to continue, extend once, suspend, or stop.
7. Record the tenant lifecycle decision.

A pilot does not convert automatically into a subscription.

---

## 13. Continuation Position

RenewalDesk does not currently have a recurring subscription offer.

Possible outcomes after the pilot are:

- End access
- Approve one explicit extension
- Offer another manually billed period
- Define a recurring-price hypothesis
- Stop commercial work
- Continue packaging based on demonstrated demand

Recurring subscription software remains premature until the shared
platform billing gate is satisfied.
