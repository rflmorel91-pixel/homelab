# Workflow Automation Package Discovery Questionnaire

Version: 1.0  
Status: Initial operating questionnaire  
Last updated: 2026-08-26

---

## 1. Purpose

Use this questionnaire during a 20–30 minute discovery call to determine whether a prospect has one bounded workflow that FieldLookers can assess, quote, implement, test, document, and hand off.

The objective is to distinguish:

- General interest from an actionable project
- A workflow problem from a feature request
- A bounded engagement from open-ended custom development
- A funded opportunity from an unfunded idea

This questionnaire supports the Workflow Automation Package:

- Fixed price: $500–$2,000
- Deposit required
- One approved workflow
- Written scope, responsibilities, exclusions, and acceptance criteria
- Ongoing support quoted separately

Do not promise price, schedule, integrations, or technical feasibility during discovery.

## 2. Call Structure

| Section | Target time |
|---|---:|
| Context and desired outcome | 2 minutes |
| Current workflow and business problem | 5 minutes |
| Trigger, inputs, outputs, and ownership | 5 minutes |
| Systems, failure points, and access | 5 minutes |
| Urgency, authority, and budget | 4 minutes |
| Acceptance, exclusions, and support | 4 minutes |
| Summary and next action | 2–5 minutes |

Prefer concrete examples from recent work over hypothetical answers.

Record the prospect's actual statements separately from FieldLookers interpretations.

### Core call rule

Do not ask every question mechanically. Complete the call using these core questions:

1. What process would you like to improve?
2. Please describe the last time it happened, step by step.
3. How often does it happen, and how much time does it consume?
4. What goes wrong, and what is the business consequence?
5. What starts the workflow?
6. What information enters, and what result must come out?
7. Who owns, performs, reviews, and approves it?
8. Which systems are involved?
9. Which cases require human judgment or must remain manual?
10. What data and access would the project require?
11. Why does this need to change now?
12. Who can approve access and the purchase?
13. Is the $500–$2,000 range and required deposit viable?
14. What observable result would prove delivery is acceptable?
15. What must be excluded from the engagement?
16. Who will own and support the workflow after handoff?

Use the detailed questions in Sections 5–13 only when a core answer is incomplete, risky, or ambiguous.

## 15. Decision Categories

### Qualified for proposal

Use only when:

- A meaningful current problem is evidenced
- One bounded workflow can be described
- An accountable owner and purchase authority are identified
- Budget range and deposit are viable
- Access and data constraints appear manageable
- Acceptance criteria can be made testable
- A clear proposal decision date or next action exists

Next action:

- Prepare a written proposal and Statement of Work
- Verify technical assumptions before committing
- Include scope, exclusions, responsibilities, price, deposit, schedule, acceptance, and support boundaries

### Qualified for further assessment

Use when the problem is credible but one or more material facts remain unresolved.

Examples:

- Integration access has not been verified
- Acceptance criteria require clarification
- A decision-maker must participate
- Data-handling requirements need review
- The workflow must be narrowed

Next action:

- Assign specific evidence to collect
- Identify the responsible person
- Set a dated follow-up
- Do not issue a fixed-price proposal yet

### Discovery only — not commercially qualified

Use when the prospect expresses interest but lacks one or more of:

- A meaningful current problem
- An identifiable workflow owner
- A defined next action
- Purchasing authority
- Viable budget
- A realistic decision timeframe

Next action:

- Record the evidence
- Continue market learning
- Do not treat the conversation as a qualified opportunity

### Decline or close

Use when:

- The workflow cannot be bounded
- Required access is unauthorized
- Data-handling risk is unacceptable
- The requested work depends on prohibited or unsupported automation
- Expectations require unlimited custom development or support
- The prospect rejects the commercial boundaries
- No active next step exists

Record the reason without retaining unnecessary sensitive information.
